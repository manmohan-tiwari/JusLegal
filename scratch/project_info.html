1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

JusLegal is an innovative mobile and web AI application built for Indian citizens. It bridges the massive gap between 1.4 billion people and the complex Indian legal framework (including the newly enacted Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita - BNS 2023, Consumer Protection Act, RTI Act, and Real Estate Regulations).

⚠️ The Problem in India
• 50+ Million Pending Court Cases: Courts are clogged, and 85% of disputes never get resolved.
• Exorbitant Lawyer Fees: Simple notices cost ₹2,000–₹5,000+, deterring common citizens.
• Fear & Legal Jargon: Citizens feel intimidated by court procedures, police stations, and complex legal terms.
✨ The JusLegal Solution
• 24/7 AI Legal Assistant: Explain issues in simple Hindi or English via voice/text.
• 60-Second Document Generator: Produce official ready-to-print legal notices, RTI apps, & complaints.
• Affordable Access: Free tier + ₹199/month PRO subscription + ₹49 on-demand drafts.
